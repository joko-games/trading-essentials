## Description

```markdown
# Trading Essentials

Trading Essentials records your auction house business for the whole account and tells you what it really earns.

- **Ledger**: every sale, purchase, listing, expiry and cancellation, with cuts and deposits. Undercut auctions are marked.
- **Profit per item**: after fees and what the units cost you, with sell-through, time to sell and a short verdict.
- **Stock**: bags, banks, Warband bank, mail and auctions for every character, shown as a bag-style grid or a list, bound items on request.
- **Gold**: curve, best and worst days, and the auction house's share.
- **At the auction house**: quick selling and a shopping list built from your tracked recipes.
- **Tooltips**: your stock, sales and profit for any item.

Uses Auctionator's prices when installed, its own scan otherwise. `/tradingessentials` opens the window.

Available in English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Korean and Chinese. Bugs and ideas: [GitHub](https://github.com/star-racer/trading-essentials/issues).
```
